Canadian Energy Stock & Economic Indicators Analysis

Ah, the Canadian energy sector – where oil flows, stocks rise (and sometimes fall faster than our hopes on Monday morning 😅), and macroeconomic indicators silently dictate the rhythm. This project dives into the relationship between oil prices, inflation, unemployment, and how they affect energy stocks like CNQ, ENB, and TRP.

✨ Objectives

  1. Figure out if oil prices are really the puppet masters behind stock moves 🛢️📈
  2. Uncover how GDP, inflation, and unemployment sneak into market trends
  3. Visualize the chaos beautifully – because graphs make everything better 😎

🧐 Key Insights

  • Oil prices and energy stocks are basically best friends with benefits.
  • GDP and inflation like to play the long game with delayed impacts.
  • Unemployment? Not as flirty with stocks as we expected.
