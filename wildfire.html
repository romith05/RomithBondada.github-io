Alberta Wildfire Ignition Prediction Dashboard

Wildfires are one of Alberta’s most severe natural hazards. This project delivers an interactive web application that predicts wildfire risk in real time by integrating weather APIs, vegetation maps, water body data, and a Random Forest machine learning model. With just a click on the map, users get a wildfire probability score within seconds.

✨ Objectives

  1. Provide an interactive wildfire risk map for Alberta 🗺️🔥
  2. Integrate geospatial (GeoTIFF), weather, and vegetation data seamlessly
  3. Deploy a containerized pipeline for fast, reproducible predictions

🧐 Key Insights

  • Random Forest Classifier achieves 94% accuracy and 0.97 recall for fire events.
  • Real-time weather data (temperature, humidity, wind speed) enriches predictions.
  • Water bodies are instantly flagged as zero risk to avoid false alarms.
